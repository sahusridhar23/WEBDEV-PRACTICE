Honey Chilly Potato

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Cornflour
  • Maida (optional)
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Garlic
  • Green chili
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Soy sauce
  • Red chili sauce
  • Tomato ketchup
  • Honey
  • Vinegar
  • Spring onion

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into thin strips.
  2. Coat with cornflour, a little maida, and salt.
  3. Deep fry until golden and crispy.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and green chili.
  5. Add onion and capsicum and stir fry briefly.
  6. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, ketchup, honey, and vinegar. Mix well.
  7. Add fried potatoes and toss to coat.
  8. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.
