Paneer Tikka

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Thick curd (yogurt)
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander powder
  • Garam masala
  • Kasuri methi
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt
  • Oil or butter
  • Capsicum
  • Onion

Instructions

  1. Cut paneer, capsicum, and onion into cubes.
  2. In a bowl mix curd, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt, lemon juice, and a little oil.
  3. Add paneer and vegetables to the marinade. Mix well.
  4. Let it marinate for 20-30 minutes.
  5. Skewer paneer and vegetables (or place on tray).
  6. Grill, bake, or cook on a pan until slightly charred.
  7. Brush with butter and serve hot.
