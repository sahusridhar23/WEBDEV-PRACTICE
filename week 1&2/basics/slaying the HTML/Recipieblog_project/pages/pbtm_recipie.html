Paneer Butter Masala

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Cashews
  • Fresh cream
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander powder
  • Garam masala
  • Salt
  • Kasuri methi

Instructions

  1. Heat butter and oil in a pan. Sauté chopped onion until soft.
  2. Add ginger and garlic. Cook briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes and cashews. Cook until soft.
  4. Cool slightly and blend into a smooth paste.
  5. Heat butter again. Add the paste and cook.
  6. Add turmeric, red chili, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt.
  7. Add a little water and simmer for a few minutes.
  8. Add paneer cubes and cook briefly.
  9. Add cream and kasuri methi. Mix and cook for 1 minute.Garnish and serve hot.
